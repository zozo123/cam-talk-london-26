## Abstract

Hill-climbing workloads — coding agents, RL post-training, simulation campaigns, computational biology, and hardware-in-the-loop — share one loop: propose, isolate, measure, keep or revert, promote once. What they lack is a named systems contract for the runtime that hosts that loop. We propose *Fork, Reduce, Promote*: fork samples parallel worlds from an immutable parent snapshot; reduce pools structured worker evidence under a frozen objective digest; promote spends a singular authority bit and burns the runners. Objective mutations are epoch bumps, not in-child patches. Path-integral CI means merge consumes a reduced measure over forks, not a green badge from one contaminated machine; world-diff protocols treat sibling divergence as evidence rather than last-writer-wins. The contribution is a **capability contract** — Search≠Authority, immutable \(S_0\), one promote bit per Epoch, structured evidence or reject, abstain under unresolved correlation — that bindings such as microVM CoW, effect-trace sessions, and workflow Cells can implement underneath. We explicitly do **not** claim that every software-factory or orchestrator path already ships native N-way fork-merge; oracle scarcity and tip sociology change by domain, while the API slots do not.
