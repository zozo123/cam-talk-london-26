\begin{frame}{Why I ended up here}
\begin{columns}[T,onlytextwidth]
\column{0.30\textwidth}
\textbf{Computational science}\par
\normalsize An experiment is not reproducible unless the state, inputs, and provenance are reproducible.

\column{0.30\textwidth}
\textbf{Production ML + builds}\par
\normalsize Reuse everything you safely can: caches, artifacts, warm state. Rebuilding the world is expensive.

\column{0.30\textwidth}
\textbf{Coding agents}\par
\normalsize Then the developer became a program that edits files, runs shells, calls networks, retries, and forks hypotheses.
\end{columns}

\vspace{0.8em}
\begin{block}{The collision}
\centering
\large The same \textbf{state} I wanted to reuse for speed became the state I had to isolate for trust.
\end{block}

\vspace{0.35em}
\centering\large
That is the road from reproducible science and build systems to \hilite{forkable sandboxes}.
\end{frame}
