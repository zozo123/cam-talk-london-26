\begin{frame}{What I want from this room}
\large
Four systems questions I do not think we have answered cleanly yet:

\vspace{0.4em}
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \term{Executable state}: what must a first-class machine value include -- FS, memory, process tree, GPU, sockets, clock, external state?
  \item \term{Model vs world}: where is the crossover where learned imagination becomes cheaper than forked executable interaction?
  \item \term{Correlation}: how should reducers price shared ancestry instead of pretending siblings are i.i.d.?
  \item \term{Authority}: how do children inherit enough state to act without inheriting the power to grade or promote themselves?
\end{enumerate}

\vspace{0.65em}
\begin{block}{If you remember one thing}
\centering\Large \hilite{Burn the runner. Keep the proof. Fork the machine, not the trust.}
\end{block}
\end{frame}
