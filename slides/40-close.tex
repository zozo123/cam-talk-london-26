\begin{frame}{What I want from this room}
\large
Four systems questions I do not think we have answered cleanly yet:

\vspace{0.4em}
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \term{Fork semantics}: what is the right first-class ``machine value'' --- FS, memory, process tree, GPU, display, sockets?
  \item \term{Correlation}: how should reducers price shared ancestry instead of pretending siblings are i.i.d.?
  \item \term{Warmth vs isolation}: where can warm state live without becoming a credential or side channel?
  \item \term{Authority}: how do children outlive parents without inheriting the parent’s power to promote?
\end{enumerate}

\vspace{0.65em}
\begin{block}{If you remember one thing}
\centering\Large \hilite{Burn the runner. Keep the proof. Fork the machine, not the trust.}
\end{block}
\end{frame}
