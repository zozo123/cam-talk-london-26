## The argument

**Self-driving computation chooses its next execution from observed results.** Ordinary instruction execution remains underneath. IF, LOOP and CALL can implement the adaptive controller as well as its candidates. The change is a controller that directs executable exploration under a goal, constraints and a concrete acceptance rule.

The cinema analogy contrasts playing a prescribed sequence with saving reached computer state, trying private plot continuations, and choosing what to keep or discard from the observed result. The rewind covers declared computer state; it cannot undo a completed remote request or rewind the surrounding world.

The main path is **state → evidence → authority**. State determines what a continuation receives and what reuse costs. Evidence determines which behavior or statistical claim a returned result supports. Authority determines whether the controller may publish the exact accepted artifact. Forks enable private continuations; the examples explain this path rather than form a separate career survey.

The speaker's biophysics → genomics → Mobileye → Incredibuild → self-driving-computers trajectory motivates the question. Published and coauthored findings retain their own methods, denominators and team attribution.

## Narrative connections

- The paradigm creates a systems question: what state can an executable continuation reuse?
- The trajectory supplies concrete computational and verification experience, with team attribution.
- AFL++ shows useful initialized-state reuse; sampling and AI research show how extra executions can feed a decision rule.
- Genomics and three-body continuation distinguish a proposed candidate from its checked effect or connection.
- Factory records expose a precise missing behavioral assertion and a recovery failure; neither proves that live memory was necessary.
- State fidelity and resource cost determine the execution mechanism. Worktrees and a warm cache remain valid baselines.
- Candidate patches are selected or composed. Measurements of one common target use a separate numerical reducer.
- Evidence identity permits declared-duplicate rejection; lineage records shared factors; calibrated statistical information requires more than authentic receipts.
- Experiments distinguish targeted behavior, backend performance, environmental dependence and repair repeatability.
- The controller verifies the exact artifact and current authority before publication, and retains artifacts on refusal.
