\[\begin{aligned}C_{\rm cold}&=N(P+W+V)\\ C_{\rm fork}&=P+H+N(R+D+W+V)\end{aligned}\]
\[(N-1)\,P \;>\; H + N\,(R+D)\]
{\footnotesize prepare $P$, capture $H$, restore $R$, diverge $D$, work $W$, evaluate $V$. $W$ includes model calls and cancels. Each child still rebuilds its patch.\par}
