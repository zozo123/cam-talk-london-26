\note{[0:50] My background is in biological physics. In this work, we extended Cytosim to vary how many filaments a binding protein could connect. We simulated six hundred seconds and collected statistics from thirty random starts in the reported comparison. A filament became a graph node, and protein connections became edges. Higher linker valency promoted bundles and larger clusters. Configuration changed the collective structure that we measured. That is the first connection to this talk: a system's reached state and shared structure matter. Genomics then made the computational representation itself a bottleneck.}
