\qadetail{Drug mechanisms in the revised visual: abemaciclib inhibits CDK4/6 and slows cell-cycle progression; SI-2 is an experimental SRC-3 inhibitor. The remaining compounds span chemotherapy and targeted inhibitors. Researchers chose the strong/weak PD-L1 inducer pair for follow-up. This small selected laboratory search is compared with the 2021 large pooled perturbation screen, without calling either study an implementation of computational checkpoint forks. The public-language title refers to tumor-cell viability and PD-L1 expression, not measured T-cell action or clinical immune evasion. Gene activity is the slide shorthand for mRNA abundance; surface protein was also measured for the selected pair. The 2019 study and 2021 CRISPR screen use related SRC-3 inhibitors, SI-2 and SI-12 respectively, but are separate studies rather than successive stages of one dataset. The common story is candidate generation, measured screening, computational comparison and focused follow-up. Candidate-count audit: the 2019 Results section names six drugs/candidates tested across four breast-cancer cell lines: doxorubicin, paclitaxel, abemaciclib, topotecan, BEZ235 and SI-2. E0771 was also tested with cisplatin, palbociclib, niraparib and methotrexate, making ten distinct compounds in that cell model. The four models are MDA-MB-231, 4T1, MCF-7 and E0771; the extra four drugs were not reported across all four models. The unordered-pair count is an explicit combinatorial illustration: 10 times 9 divided by 2 equals 45 pairs of distinct drugs, at one chosen dose per drug and without distinguishing order or timing. It is not a count of tested combinations or a 45-pair screen. The paper describes abemaciclib plus SI-2 as the first strong/weak-inducer combination test. Dose and timing add further choices, but no unsupported number is assigned to them. The ten single-drug branches in the schematic are represented by abemaciclib, SI-2 and a grouped eight other drugs. Fan-out/fan-in describes separate treatment conditions followed by comparison and a selected-pair follow-up. It does not assert that all ten assays ran simultaneously or that biological cells were computationally forked. Parallel forks are the proposed computational analogy. Primary full text: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6805932/. In four human/mouse breast-cancer lines, drug exposure near IC50 induced PD-L1 expression. In E0771 cells, PD-L1 gene activity induction varied by drug, approximately tenfold with paclitaxel or abemaciclib and up to approximately one hundredfold with doxorubicin or topotecan. This range concerns different single-drug exposures, not the fold reduction from the combination. After 72 hours in E0771 cells, abemaciclib with SI-2 reduced PD-L1 gene activity/surface protein induction toward the weaker-inducer level while retaining cytotoxicity. The text gives no exact combination fold reduction, and the slide invents none. Addition of abemaciclib up to 625 nM did not further elevate ATF4 stress protein; retained as a separate endpoint. The March 2020 correction changes the Figure 2A label INF to IFN, not these results. These are tumor-cell marker and viability assays, not measurements of T-cell response, patient benefit, a deployed treatment policy or autonomous immunotherapy. Eliaz is a coauthor; Gilad performed experiments and Eliaz contributed analysis, visualization and study conception. The result motivates the user's response-guided immunotherapy research direction by showing why multiple measured objectives matter. It is distinct from the later SI-12 synthetic-lethal CRISPR screen.}
