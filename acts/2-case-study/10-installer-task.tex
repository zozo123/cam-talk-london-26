\note{[0:45] In software, a goal becomes a sequence of experiments. Here the goal was to let a sandbox install Python tools. An agent proposes a change, tests exercise its behavior, and review inspects the result. Failed checks or requested changes send the work back for another attempt. This is a feedback loop: measured outcomes determine what happens next. Our recorded repair ran serially.
