\note{[0:40] In our genomics work, we sought perturbations that make cancer cells more sensitive to the SRC-three inhibitor SI-twelve. The GeCKOv2 library supplied more than 120,000 guides targeting 19,050 genes. We compared dropout under the inhibitor and vehicle, then used Terrace and DRACO to rank candidates and shortlist about one hundred genes. This stage proposes promising targets. A separate assay must measure their effect.}
