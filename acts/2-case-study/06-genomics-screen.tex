\qadetail{Primary full text audited on 4 October 2026: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7994904/. Two methods (Terrace and DRACO) each selected 25 depleted candidates from each of T2 and T3, giving 2 x 2 x 25 = 100 ranked nomination slots before overlaps; the paper describes approximately 100 genes. The 190-fold narrowing is an approximate calculation 19,050 / approximately 100, not an exact unique-hit retention count. Eight targets with available pharmacological inhibitors were selected; five further genes were added for siRNA experiments. This yields 13 genetic follow-up targets, of which 10 increased sensitivity, and eight drug combinations, of which six improved killing under the paper criteria. Counts refer to stages and assay types, not one set of interchangeable samples. The pooled screen included vehicle, SI-12 and fulvestrant arms; the focused visible question is SI-12 versus vehicle. Monitoring involved guide-count sequencing, including baseline and later times; the laboratory screen preceded ranking. Do not portray computer analysis as replacing or preceding the first pooled screen. The earlier Scientific Reports study used SI-2, while the related 2021 work used SI-12; they are separate studies connected by the same search-and-validation principle.
