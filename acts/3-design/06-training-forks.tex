\begin{ticks}
\item \textbf{pause} the sandbox while the model generates, up to 98\% of its life
\item \textbf{fork} the sandbox ``for reward judging without side effects''
\item \textbf{snapshot} at intervals, for error recovery
\item \textbf{partial rollout}: update once a fraction $\lambda$ of trajectories completes, to limit long-tail latency
\end{ticks}\par}
