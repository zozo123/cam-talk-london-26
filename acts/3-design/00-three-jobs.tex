\begin{frame}[t]{Forkable execution separates running, evidence collection and acceptance.\ev{\tagP}}
\vspace{0pt}

\begin{center}\begin{tikzpicture}[x=1cm,y=1cm,every node/.style={align=center,font=\fontsize{12.5}{15}\selectfont}]
\node[state,text width=3.65cm,minimum height=1.7cm](run) at (0,0) {\textbf{Run}\\Reuse a state\\Start private continuations};
\node[evidence,text width=3.65cm,minimum height=1.7cm](evidence) at (4.8,0) {\textbf{Evidence}\\Check exact artifacts\\Record outside the child};
\node[candidate,text width=3.65cm,minimum height=1.7cm](accept) at (9.6,0) {\textbf{Accept}\\Select, compose or pool\\Authorize one result};
\draw[flow](run)--(evidence);\draw[flow](evidence)--(accept);
\end{tikzpicture}\end{center}
\claim{A child may propose a result; the controller decides what continues.}
\sources{Integrated execution contract motivated by the examples; external evidence and acceptance are design requirements.}

\qadetail{This is the architecture contract for the rest of the talk. Runtime starts private continuations and preserves the declared state surface. An evaluator/recorder outside child-controlled state binds executed checks to exact artifact digests. Acceptance selects or composes candidates after the appropriate check; numerical pooling requires a common target and a justified information/dependence model. Independence is sufficient for the reference reducer, not necessary for every valid pooling method: calibrated covariance may justify dependent observations. Unknown sharing or calibration calls for refusing an unsupported precision claim. External recording and abstention are integration design requirements, not features already implemented by the reference reducer. The controller retains publication authority and an idempotent acceptance identity; external effects require durable records and uncertainty handling rather than a blanket exactly-once guarantee.}
\note{[0:45] The examples are complete. They give the runtime three jobs. Run means reuse a declared state and start private continuations. Evidence means check the exact artifact and record what executed outside the child. Acceptance means pick or compose candidates, or pool measurements under a justified model, then authorize the result. A child cannot supply its own final approval or statistical weight. We will work through the execution boundary first, then the evidence boundary, and end with the acceptance contract.}
\end{frame}
