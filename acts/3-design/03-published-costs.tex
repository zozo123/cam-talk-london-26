\note{[0:45] Two published figures show why the operation label matters. DeltaBox reports a ten point eight three millisecond checkpoint. SnowFlock reports six hundred to eight hundred milliseconds for a remote fork across hosts. One saves state; the other makes a continuation available on another host. The broader source table also includes minimum checkpoint and resume times, local forks, and boot to application code. A useful comparison matches the usable state and timing boundary. Our API trace therefore names the complete operation it actually executed.}
