Potemkin cloned honeypots and timed each clone to its first reply. SnowFlock cloned across hosts. Catalyzer forked a sandbox, and MITOSIS forked remotely over RDMA. Agent checkpointing systems appeared this year. Recovery systems handle failures and external effects. Their reported numbers measure different operations.}
