\claim{Assign a new observation ID to a fresh outcome and retain its case and ancestry.}
\sources{Illustrative evaluation ledger; replicate identities in Gilad et al., Communications Biology (2021).}
\qadetail{A copied receipt remains the same observation. A genuinely fresh stochastic outcome can obtain a new observation ID but retains the case, candidate and ancestry cluster. Deterministic reruns supply execution checks, not new test cases. Four runs of one hundred cases do not establish four hundred independent cases. Distinct worker declarations do not authenticate underlying evidence.}
\note{[0:50] Consider one hundred benchmark cases, each evaluated in four runs. We collect four hundred outcomes but retain one hundred case identities. A fresh stochastic evaluation gets a new observation identifier and keeps its case, candidate and ancestry labels. Those labels let us ask whether our variation comes from cases, repeated measurements or related continuations. The genomics case used the same distinction between technical repeats and independent experiments. A ledger preserves those relationships so that later analysis can use the appropriate unit. Next, we quantify one consequence of shared variation.}
