\note{[0:45] A reducer is the code that combines returned numerical summaries. Our reference reducer gives every declared observation an identifier. If observation forty-two arrives twice, it rejects the duplicate. If observations forty-two and forty-three arrive, it combines their summaries and retains their IDs and source history. That history records where results came from. The current numerical model assumes independent estimates of one quantity.
