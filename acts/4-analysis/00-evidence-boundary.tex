\begin{frame}[t]{Evidence must identify what ran and what the runs shared.}
\vspace{0pt}

\vspace{.45cm}
\lead{\textbf{Runtime} starts and copies execution environments.}
\vspace{.2cm}
\lead{\textbf{Evidence} records checks, artifacts and shared observations.}
\vspace{.2cm}
\lead{\textbf{Acceptance} authorizes the exact result outside the child.}
\sources{The same run--evidence--acceptance contract; distinct execution does not establish distinct information.}

\qadetail{This bridge states the responsibilities without repeating the component-architecture diagram. Two private executions can use the same test fixture, prompt, grader, remote response or recorded observation. Artifact selection, exact composition and common-target measurement pooling have different checks, as the following menu explains. The integrated external recorder and abstaining evaluator are proposed; the built reducer carries declared evidence identity and lineage under its stated statistical model.}
\note{[0:30] Private execution separates writable state. It does not establish independent information. Two runs may share an observation or a grader, even with different parents. Runtime records how environments execute; evidence records the checks and shared inputs; acceptance controls the final effect. We now distinguish choosing one artifact, composing several artifacts, and pooling measurements.}
\end{frame}
