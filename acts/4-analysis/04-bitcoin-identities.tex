The address-linking method reported greater-than99percent sensitivity and specificity in its validation. Early-mining identity inference is methodological background, not evidence about execution forks or agent accuracy. }
\note{[0:45] A prior collaboration provides another reason to separate record identity from source identity. We linked early Bitcoin addresses and recovered sixty-four agents who mined most Bitcoin during the studied early period. Many visible addresses represented fewer underlying actors. The connection here is an accounting principle: distinct identifiers need not mean independent sources. This study does not measure correlations between forks. It motivates preserving both an observation's identity and the relationships that may connect it to other observations.}
