\note{[0:50] Averaging helps because independent errors can cancel. If a hundred estimates each have noise sigma and independent errors, their average has one tenth that noise. Now let every pair of errors have correlation point one. Some errors move together, so they do not cancel. The average has about one third of the original noise, equivalent in mean variance to nine point one seven independent estimates. With an unchanged signal, the signal-to-noise gain is about three, rather than ten. This is a precision calculation under a specified model, not a claim that the estimates are unbiased in practice.}
