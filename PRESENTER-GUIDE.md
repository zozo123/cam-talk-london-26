### 10. Forkable computation lets us try different futures from the same state.

*0:50; starts 6:00*

Think of a film with a fixed sequence of scenes. Rewind lets us replay it, but the plot stays the same. Now imagine saving a reached state and trying two different next actions. ### 48. For estimates of one quantity, covariance guides the pooling weights.
